Arrow Drift privacy policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Arrow Drift is an arrow-path puzzle provided by its developer. This policy covers the Android app com.ranjitsahoo.arrowdrift. Privacy contact: appsupportdesk.in@gmail.com.

Game progress on your device

The game stores level progress, stars, scores, settings, daily and weekly completion, streaks, theme unlocks and hint/ad-completion counters on your device. There is no game account, sign-in, chat, purchase or cloud leaderboard. We do not operate a gameplay-data server. Core puzzles work offline. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may remove local progress; Android backup/restore may retain app data according to your device settings.

Advertising and consent

The app includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform for ad privacy choices. The current development build uses Google test ad identifiers, which do not generate advertising revenue. Ads and extra ad-supported hints require network access; core gameplay does not. There are no board banners. Full-screen ads may appear only between completed boards, at most once per three completions. Optional rewarded ads may be offered for extra hints: one free hint per level, then two completed ads for each of two additional hints. Watching is voluntary and normal play remains available if you decline.

Google advertising services may collect or share IP addresses and inferred approximate location, ad/app interactions, diagnostic/performance data, advertising IDs, app-set IDs and other identifiers. These can be used to serve and measure ads, provide analytics to the ad service, and prevent fraud. We do not request your precise GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. Ad traffic is handled by Google and its advertising partners, subject to their policies and the choices available in your region. We do not sell gameplay records ourselves.

When the configured consent service requires a choice, it may show a consent form before ads can load. Available ad privacy choices can be reopened from Settings. You may also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Some data may still be processed for limited ads, security or fraud prevention, as permitted by law. Declining an optional hint ad does not prevent solving the puzzle.

Google privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy How Google uses information from apps using its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear it or it is removed through uninstall/backup settings. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete that local data. There is no game account to delete. Data processed by Google is retained under Google's policies; we do not control Google's retention periods. To ask about a support message or information you sent directly to us, email the privacy contact. We keep support correspondence only as needed to answer and follow up, handle legal obligations or disputes, then delete it when no longer needed.

Security

The game does not transmit saved puzzle progress to a developer server. Google describes its ad data transmissions as encrypted with TLS. No system can promise complete security. Do not send passwords, card details or other sensitive information in a support request.

Children and audience

This is a general-audience puzzle, not a child-directed learning app. It is not designed specifically for children under 13. Parents should supervise use where needed. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate. Google Play's content rating is determined separately by its rating questionnaire.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. The effective date identifies the version. Questions or privacy requests: appsupportdesk.in@gmail.com.
